# AlphaSense

## 1. Snapshot
- **Owner/parent:** Independent (AlphaSense, Inc.), venture/PE-backed. **HQ:** New York City (flagship HQ at Hudson Yards; also founded/rooted in Finland). **Founded:** 2011 by Jack Kokko (CEO) and Raj Neervannan. **Positioning:** AI-powered market-intelligence and enterprise-search platform over a 500M+ document universe.
- **M&A:** Acquired **Sentieo** (May 2022, financial-research platform, ~1,000 customers added) and **Tegus** (announced Jun 2024, closed Jul 8 2024, ~$930M — added expert-interview library + Canalyst models + BamSEC). These are the cluster's two headline mergers.
- **Regions/markets covered:** Global. Broad international document coverage (filings, broker research, news) across US and foreign companies; strongest on developed-market equities and enterprise content.
- **Free tier:** No — enterprise subscription only; trials/demos via sales.
- **Pricing model & known ranges:** Per-seat annual subscription. Reported ~$10,000–$20,000 per seat/yr; average enterprise deals $50,000–$100,000+, largest customers >$1M/yr. **Officially not publicly disclosed.** Company reported surpassing $400M ARR (Mar 2025); valuation reached ~$4B (2024) and reportedly ~$7.5B (2026 round).

## 2. Data-domain coverage
- **fundamentals:** Via Sentieo/Canalyst heritage — standardized financials and models within the platform.
- **filings:** Deep — SEC and global filings, with semantic search and redlining.
- **transcripts/expert calls:** Very deep — earnings-call transcripts plus Tegus's expert-interview library (150,000+ transcripts).
- **qualitative research:** Core strength — broker/sell-side research, news, company docs, trade press; 500M+ documents.
- **private-company data:** Some via Tegus expert coverage of private companies (35,000+ public and private companies).
- **entity/knowledge graph:** Semantic tagging/entity linking underpins search; not sold as a standalone graph.

## 3. Datasets
- 500M+ premium documents: broker research, expert-call transcripts, company filings/presentations, earnings transcripts, news and trade journals, plus customers' own internal content.
- Tegus expert-interview library (150,000+ investor-led interviews across 35,000+ companies); Canalyst financial models; BamSEC SEC-filing search.
- **Sourcing method:** Licensing (broker research, news), primary expert interviews (Tegus network), filing ingestion, plus proprietary NLP tagging; multi-year history.
- **Proprietary holdings:** The expert-transcript corpus, Canalyst model library, and the semantic search/entity index.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- **APIs:** Developer platform (developer.alpha-sense.com) with search/management APIs and an **Agent API** exposing GenSearch, ThinkLonger and DeepResearch to AI agents.
- **MCP:** Yes — AlphaSense exposes **MCP tools/connectors** for agent integration (publishes guidance on "MCP in production").
- **Enterprise Intelligence / Private Cloud:** Deploys AlphaSense search/summarization/chat over a customer's own proprietary content.
- **AI/LLM features:** AlphaSense Assistant (GenAI chat over documents), generative search, summarization, smart synonyms/sentiment.
- **Formats/delivery:** Web app, API, enterprise/private-cloud; integrations into customer workflows.

## 5. Enabling technology
- Proprietary NLP/semantic search ("Smart Synonyms"), document sentiment, entity recognition and summarization; large-scale LLM layer for Assistant/GenSearch/DeepResearch.
- Retrieval architecture combining licensed + expert + internal content with source citation.
- Human-curated expert network (Tegus heritage) provides human-in-the-loop primary content.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- **Ratings:** G2 ~4.6/5 across ~346 reviews; broadly positive across independent reviews (IntuitionLabs, AI Flow Review).
- **Pros:** Fast, accurate semantic search; huge premium-content universe; strong summarization/GenAI; time-savings for research.
- **Cons:** High cost; steep learning curve / interface can overwhelm new users; enterprise-only.
- **User segments:** Majority of S&P 500 corporates, most large investment firms and global banks, corporate strategy, consulting; buy-side and sell-side research.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- **Leads:** Breadth of premium + expert + internal content unified under best-in-class AI search; the consolidation of Sentieo, Tegus, Canalyst and BamSEC makes it the category's most complete research suite.
- **Lags:** Price and complexity; not a lightweight/API-first data feed; fundamentals depth secondary to search/qualitative strength.
- **Best for:** Enterprises and institutional investors wanting one AI search layer over filings, broker research, expert calls, news and their own documents.
