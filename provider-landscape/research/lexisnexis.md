# LexisNexis (Nexis Diligence+ / Risk Solutions)

## 1. Snapshot
- Owner/parent: **RELX plc** (LSE/AEX/NYSE: REL). LexisNexis Legal & Professional publishes **Nexis Diligence+**; **LexisNexis Risk Solutions** publishes the compliance/screening line (WorldCompliance, Bridger). HQ: New York / Miamisburg, Ohio (Risk Solutions in Alpharetta, GA & Dayton). LexisNexis roots to 1970s (Mead Data Central); WorldCompliance acquired 2013.
- Positioning one-liner: enhanced due-diligence, third-party risk and adverse-media/sanctions screening built on one of the world's largest licensed news, legal and compliance content archives.
- **Regions/markets covered:** Global. Very strong US/EU coverage (legal records, litigation, news); broad Asia/EMEA/LatAm via multilingual news and the WorldCompliance global watchlist database; localized portals (en-us, en-int, en-gb, en-za).
- **Free tier:** No (demo/trial via sales only).
- Pricing model & known ranges: Subscription, per-user + datasets. Real published figures (UK G-Cloud): **£350/mo (1 user), £600/mo (2), £1,300/mo (5), £2,500/mo (10 users)**, including ~600 searches/user/year; 10+ users POA. Risk Solutions (WorldCompliance/Bridger) pricing not publicly disclosed.

## 2. Data-domain coverage
- Company reference/firmographics: yes — global company records, corporate filings, D&B-style profiles inside Diligence+.
- Beneficial ownership: yes — UBO data and entity-relationship trees (recursive nth-degree links).
- KYC/identity: partial — via Risk Solutions (Bridger, InstantID) more than Nexis Diligence+ itself.
- Sanctions/PEP/adverse-media: **core strength** — WorldCompliance database of watchlisted individuals/entities/vessels across 50+ risk categories; 35,000+ news sources for adverse media.
- Private-company sourcing: no (not a deal-origination tool).
- Credit/risk scores: partial — risk categorization/flags, not bureau credit scores.

## 3. Datasets
- Nexis content: tens of thousands of global news, legal, trade, and company sources (LexisNexis cites 35,000+ news sources for adverse media; broader Nexis archive spans legal cases, licensed newspapers, company/financial reports).
- WorldCompliance database: ~1.8M+ detailed profiles (individuals, entities, vessels) across 50+ risk categories/subcategories; PEPs, sanctions, enforcement, adverse media.
- History depth: deep archival news/legal back-files (decades for major sources).
- Proprietary IDs/graph: entity-relationship mapping / relationship trees curated by LexisNexis research staff, plus UBO linkage.
- Sourcing method: licensed publisher feeds + proprietary research/curation + structured watchlist collection.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- Nexis Diligence+ is primarily a web application with an automated report builder; **Nexis Data-as-a-Service / Web Services Kit (WSK)** APIs available for bulk content licensing.
- Risk Solutions offers screening APIs (WorldCompliance data feed, Bridger real-time screening) — REST/SOAP; batch and real-time.
- Auth: API keys / enterprise credentials; formats typically XML/JSON.
- Delivery: web UI, API, and bulk data feed for the WorldCompliance database (also resold via partners e.g. Infocredit).
- MCP availability: none published.

## 5. Enabling technology
- Entity resolution and recursive relationship-tree building over individuals/companies/vessels.
- Adverse-media analytics with multilingual news processing and negativity/relevance filtering.
- AI/ML: name-matching, risk categorization, article de-duplication and relevancy scoring.
- Compliance tooling: audit-ready report builder, ongoing monitoring/alerts, customer-risk profiling for CDD/EDD workflows.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- G2: Nexis Diligence+ listed with generally positive marks; WorldCompliance Search Tool reviewed separately (both modest review counts, ~4/5 range). Capterra hosts a Nexis Diligence+ profile (limited public review volume).
- Triangulated sentiment (G2, Capterra, FitGap): **Pros** — breadth and authority of licensed content, defensible/auditable diligence reports, strong sanctions/PEP/adverse-media depth. **Cons** — dated/complex UI vs newer tools, cost, and volume of results requiring manual filtering.
- User segments: compliance, legal, risk, procurement teams in banks, corporates, law firms.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- Leads on: authoritative, licensed global news + legal content depth, sanctions/PEP/adverse-media (WorldCompliance), and audit-defensible EDD reports — a compliance/reputational-risk standard.
- Lags on: modern UX, deal-sourcing/private-company discovery, and self-serve/low-cost access.
- Best-for: regulated institutions needing documented enhanced due diligence and screening with strong legal/media provenance.
