# CB Insights

## 1. Snapshot
- **Owner/parent:** Independent (private; venture-backed, incl. National Science Foundation origins). HQ New York, NY; founded 2008 by Anand Sanwal and Jonathan Sherry.
- **Scale signal:** Enterprise/tech-market intelligence used by corporates, VCs, and strategy teams; "Business Graph" of 10m+ companies. Known for widely-cited research (unicorn lists, State of Venture, State of AI).
- **Positioning one-liner:** "Predictive intelligence on private companies / the technology insights platform" — signal-driven tracking of startups, venture funding, and emerging tech.
- **Pricing model:** No public list price; quote-only annual. Approx. (buyer sites): Essential/individual ~$29,800/yr; scaling to $100,000+/yr for Enterprise with API, CRM integration, unlimited seats. Floor commonly cited ~$50k/yr.

## 2. Asset-class coverage
- **Public equity:** Minimal — tracks public tech companies as part of the graph but not an equity-research/markets tool.
- **Private equity / VC:** Core strength — **venture/tech-startup** focus: funding rounds (VC/PE/M&A/IPO), investors, valuations, deal activity, emerging-tech taxonomy. Skews earlier-stage/tech vs PitchBook's broader PE.
- **Fixed income & credit:** Not covered — no credit/fixed-income franchise.
- **Other (funds/LP-GP, real assets, ESG, alt data):** Heavy on **alternative signals** — patents, news, hiring/headcount, web traffic, partnerships/clients/vendors, product/tech relationships. Not an LP/GP or real-assets source.

## 3. Datasets
- Coverage: **10m+ private & public companies** in the Business Graph — firmographics, funding history, and **business relationships** (partnerships, clients, vendors, licensing, competitors).
- **Mosaic Score:** proprietary 0–1,000 private-company health score across four dimensions (**Momentum, Market, Money, Management**); NSF-developed; marketed as **4.7x more predictive** than top VCs at spotting future unicorns. Also Commercial Maturity and other signal scores.
- **Sourcing method:** machine-learning/NLP over **patents, news, startup websites, hiring data, VC financings, and public filings** — an algorithm-first, signal-heavy approach (vs PitchBook/Preqin's human-research emphasis).

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- **API / data feeds** for company and market insight (request-based pricing on enterprise/data plans).
- Delivery integrations: **Snowflake, Salesforce, Microsoft Copilot, Excel, Zapier**, and MCP-style integrations.
- Platform features: company profiles, predictive scores, AI-generated insights, "Browser/Analyst" workflows, visual analytics/reports.
- Auth token-based; formats JSON/feeds. Entitlement by seats + modules; API/CRM integrations gated to higher tiers; redistribution restricted (enterprise terms).

## 5. Enabling technology
- **Algorithm-/ML-first** engine: NLP entity extraction and classification across news, patents, filings, web and hiring data; auto-updating firmographics and relationship graph.
- Entity resolution across millions of private companies (dedup, linking signals to entities, mapping tech/market taxonomies).
- **AI/LLM features:** generative "ChatCBI"-style assistant and AI-generated insight summaries; strong emphasis on predictive scoring (Mosaic) as the differentiator; positions as an AI/analyst-augmentation platform.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- **Ratings (triangulated):** G2 and TrustRadius listings (pages partly egress-blocked this run); corroborated via CompareCamp, Tomba, subscribed.fyi, RFP.wiki — generally solid but with recurring cost/depth caveats.
- **PROS:** strong **visual analytics + research reports**; useful for **trend/emerging-tech spotting**, competitive intelligence, and disruption tracking; Mosaic predictive scores valued; good for corporate strategy/innovation and VC sourcing narratives.
- **CONS:** **opaque, high pricing**; steep learning curve; **data-freshness/coverage gaps in niche verticals**; deal/valuation granularity and financials less complete than PitchBook; more "insight/signal" than exhaustive transactional data.
- **Segments:** corporate strategy/innovation/R&D, corporate development/M&A, VCs, consultants, market/competitive-intelligence teams.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- **Leads:** **emerging-tech and predictive signal intelligence** on private tech companies — Mosaic scores, alternative-data signals (hiring, patents, partnerships), and high-visibility research. Moat = ML/NLP signal engine + brand research + relationship graph tuned for early-stage tech.
- **Lags:** transactional deal/fund/LP depth vs **PitchBook** and **Preqin**; no credit/fixed-income or LP-GP data; not a comprehensive PE/buyout or fund-performance source; cost hard to justify for pure deal-database needs.
