# Preqin

## 1. Snapshot
- **Owner/parent:** **BlackRock** — agreed to acquire Preqin June 2024 for **£2.55bn / ~$3.2bn cash**; deal closed early 2025. Preqin sits alongside/into BlackRock's **Aladdin** ecosystem but continues to be offered as a standalone solution. HQ London; founded 2003; offices across US, Europe, Asia.
- **Scale signal:** 200,000+ users (asset managers, insurers, pensions, wealth managers, banks, service providers); one of the largest independent private-markets + hedge-fund datasets.
- **Positioning one-liner:** "The home of alternatives" — the default data platform for LP-side due diligence and alternatives fundraising/benchmarking.
- **Pricing model:** No public list price; seat + asset-class + module based. Approx. benchmarks: Preqin Pro single-asset-class ~$2,000/yr/user, multi-asset ~$4,000+/yr/user (small); typical org contracts ~$25k–$80k/yr. **Data Feeds/API** start ~$50k/yr, enterprise feeds with redistribution can exceed $150k/yr.

## 2. Asset-class coverage
- **Public equity:** Not a focus — Preqin is an alternatives specialist; public equity essentially out of scope.
- **Private equity / VC:** Core strength. Funds, fund managers (GPs), investors (LPs), fundraising, deals, portfolio companies, dry powder, performance.
- **Fixed income & credit:** Strong **private debt / private credit** coverage (a headline asset class): funds, managers, performance, deals. Not public/liquid fixed income.
- **Other (funds/LP-GP, real assets, ESG, alt data):** Deep — real estate, infrastructure, natural resources, **hedge funds**, secondaries, funds of funds. Signature strength in **LP–GP relationships, fund performance (IRR/TVPI/DPI/cash flows), benchmarks**, ESG data, service-provider directory.

## 3. Datasets
- Coverage (approx., Preqin-published): **~190,000+ funds** (235,000+ cited incl. all asset classes), **~60,000 fund managers**, **~30,000+ institutional investors/LPs**; private-company financials, deals/transactions, ESG, service providers.
- **Performance depth:** 20+ years of net-of-fee IRR, TVPI, DPI and cash-flow data; transparent return metrics on 15,000+ private-capital funds and 30,000+ hedge funds — widely seen as the deepest LP-reported performance set.
- **Benchmarks:** Preqin Benchmarks (PME, quartile) a differentiator.
- **Sourcing method:** distinctive reliance on **Freedom-of-Information (FOIA) requests to public pensions**, voluntary GP/LP submissions, self-reported surveys, public filings, and manual analyst research — the FOIA/public-LP angle underpins its performance transparency.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- **Preqin Pro** (web app) is the primary interface.
- **Operational API** — programmatic query access for building lists/workflows into internal systems.
- **Feeds API / Data Feeds** — bulk transfer optimized, **CSV streams**, 100+ feeds covering full datasets for warehousing (e.g., used with DataOps tools like Rivery).
- **Marketplace delivery:** Preqin Data listed on **Snowflake Marketplace** and **AWS**.
- Auth: API keys/tokens; formats CSV (feeds) / JSON (operational API). Entitlement by asset class + seats; API/feeds priced separately with **redistribution** governed by enterprise license terms.

## 5. Enabling technology
- Analyst-driven research operations combined with automated ingestion (filings, FOIA responses, survey pipelines) and manual verification.
- Entity resolution across GPs/LPs/funds/deals; relationship mapping (investor↔fund↔manager).
- Post-BlackRock: integration path with **Aladdin** for private-markets portfolio/risk workflows; AI/analytics roadmap emerging but historically a research-and-data house rather than an AI-first product.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- **Ratings (triangulated):** Reviewed across G2 / TrustRadius / SourceForge / Slashdot — generally positive on data breadth; specific G2/TrustRadius pages partly inaccessible this run, corroborated via review aggregators and independent reviews (e.g., Adam Fayed review, comparison guides).
- **PROS:** unmatched **LP/GP + fund-performance** data; the "default" for LP-side due diligence at pensions/endowments/allocators; strong benchmarks; trustworthy multi-source methodology; broad alternatives coverage in one place.
- **CONS:** private-company/**deal-level** data less deep than PitchBook; some coverage gaps outside North America / for managers who don't self-report; UI and search can feel dated; expensive; reliance on self-reported/FOIA data means selective completeness.
- **Segments:** LPs (pensions, endowments, insurers, wealth), GPs/fundraising & IR teams, fund-of-funds, consultants, placement agents.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- **Leads:** **LP–GP intelligence and fund performance/benchmarks** across all private-alternatives asset classes (PE, VC, private debt, real estate, infra, natural resources, hedge funds). Moat = 20+ years of FOIA/self-reported performance data + LP relationship coverage that deal-centric rivals lack; now backstopped by BlackRock/Aladdin distribution.
- **Lags:** deal/company-transaction granularity vs **PitchBook**; venture/emerging-tech signal vs **CB Insights**; no public-markets story; UI/AI modernization behind newer entrants.
