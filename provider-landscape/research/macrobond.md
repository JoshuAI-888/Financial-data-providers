# Macrobond

## 1. Snapshot
- **Owner/parent:** Owned by **Francisco Partners** (acquired from Nordic Capital in July 2023; Nordic Capital had been majority owner since 2018). Founded **2008** in **Malmö, Sweden**; offices across Europe, North America and Asia.
- **Positioning one-liner:** The modern all-in-one desktop + API platform for macro/financial research — aggregate, transform, chart and publish millions of series from 2,400+ sources in one governed environment.
- **Regions/coverage:** Global. Aggregates macro, financial and industry data from **2,400+ sources**, giving broad DM and EM reach including Asia and Europe; AU/NZ headline and financial series covered via official sources. Breadth is source-aggregation rather than deep proprietary local-EM curation (where CEIC leads).
- **Free tier:** No — subscription only, though a **free trial** is available.
- **Pricing model & known ranges:** Three editions — **Lite, Pro, Enterprise** — quote-based, **not publicly disclosed**. Independent estimates put per-seat licences in the low-to-mid **thousands of USD per user per year** (well below Bloomberg/Refinitiv terminal cost), scaling with data modules and API.

## 2. Data-domain coverage
- **Macro indicators:** Yes — comprehensive macroeconomic series across countries, a core strength.
- **Central-bank/rates:** Yes — policy rates, yield curves, monetary/financial data.
- **Trade:** Yes — trade and balance-of-payments series from official sources.
- **Commodities:** Yes — commodity prices and energy/industry data.
- **Forecasts:** Partial — hosts third-party/consensus forecasts and enables users to build their own; not primarily a proprietary-forecast house.
- **Country risk:** Partial — supplies indicators and financial data for risk analysis rather than a packaged rating.
- **High-frequency/alt-macro:** Yes — market/financial and higher-frequency series, plus growing alternative datasets.

## 3. Datasets
- Millions of time series aggregated from **2,400+ global, official and proprietary sources**; broad macro + financial + industry coverage in one database.
- History depth follows source availability (long official histories) with **full revision history / vintages** retained.
- Update frequency: continuous, source-driven; strong intraday/market plus scheduled macro releases.
- Sourcing: aggregation of official statistics, central banks, markets and third-party vendors — not proprietary primary collection.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- **API type:** Windows **desktop application** plus a **Data+ API** with official **Python** and **R** libraries, an **Excel add-in**, and web/embed publishing; MATLAB support.
- **Auth:** Subscription credentials / API keys.
- **Formats:** Native objects via Python/R libraries, Excel, chart/image and web-embed outputs, JSON via API.
- **Rate limits:** Governed by subscription tier.
- **Delivery:** Desktop, API (Python/R), Excel, and publishing/embeds; enterprise data-delivery options. No widely advertised first-party Snowflake/Databricks share or public MCP server (integration is via its own API/libraries).

## 5. Enabling technology
- Built on a **governed data foundation with full lineage, revision history and vintages** — a marketed differentiator.
- Strong **harmonisation and transformation** engine: automatic unit/frequency alignment, calculations, and **self-updating charts** that refresh as data revises.
- Investing in AI/assistant features and modern data-ops (discovery, management, analysis, publishing lifecycle) across Desktop and API.
- Emphasis on integrations (e.g. Bloomberg and other sources) feeding into one analytical environment.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- Sentiment (G2 ~4.7/5, practitioner reviews): praised for **ease of use, self-updating charts, powerful transformations and time saved** in cross-country analysis; strong charting/publishing.
- Cons: **learning curve**, occasional **slow performance on very large databases/complex charts**, and it **lacks the deep market/security data of Bloomberg** (macro-first, not a full markets terminal).
- User segments: macro economists, strategists, asset allocators, and research/publishing teams at banks, asset managers and corporates.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- **Leads on:** modern UX, aggregation breadth, revision/vintage governance, and best-in-class charting/publishing + Python/R workflow.
- **Lags on:** deep security-level market data (vs Bloomberg/LSEG) and the most granular proprietary EM local-source data (vs CEIC); premium (non-free) price.
- **Best for:** research teams that want one governed platform to discover, transform, visualise and publish global macro/financial data.
- **Free pick?** No — but far cheaper than a terminal; a strong paid mid-market choice.
