# PitchBook

## 1. Snapshot
- **Owner/parent:** Morningstar, Inc. (acquired remaining stake in 2016). HQ Seattle, WA; founded 2007 by John Gabbert. Additional offices London, NYC, SF, Hong Kong.
- **Scale signal:** 100,000+ professional users worldwide; ~3,000+ employees incl. a large in-house research/data-collection operation. Positioned as the leading **private + public capital-markets** database (VC, PE, M&A) with a full-lifecycle "one platform" pitch.
- **Positioning one-liner:** "The data, research and technology covering the full breadth of private and public capital markets" — private-market deal/company/investor intelligence, now extended into credit via LCD.
- **Pricing model:** No public list price; direct sales, seat-based (named users) + module add-ons (LCD/Credit, API, Data Feed all priced separately). Known ranges (approx., triangulated from buyer-data sites): solo/single seat ~$15k–$20k/yr; small team base ~$25k for 3 seats, additional seats ~$7k/yr each; per-user effective $4.9k–$13.5k/yr; enterprise $70k–$124k+/yr. Median annual contract value cited ~$30k (114 verified buyer transactions). LCD/Credit, API and Data Feed carry material incremental fees.

## 2. Asset-class coverage
- **Public equity:** Present but not the draw — public-company financials, comps, and public-comparable benchmarking exist, but PitchBook is chosen for **private**, not public equity (Bloomberg/CapIQ/FactSet lead public).
- **Private equity / VC:** Core strength and market leader. Deep coverage of VC, growth, PE/buyout, M&A — deals, valuations, cap tables, multiples, ownership, exits, fund returns, LP commitments. Widely regarded #1 for US/global VC deal data.
- **Fixed income & credit:** Materially strengthened via **LCD (Leveraged Commentary & Data)**, acquired from S&P Global (closed 2022, ~$650m incl. contingent). Real-time US + European leveraged-loan and high-yield coverage, Morningstar LSTA/European Leveraged Loan Indexes, plus newer **BDC and CLO datasets** and private-credit intelligence under "PitchBook Credit."
- **Other (funds/LP-GP, real assets, ESG, alt data):** Funds of funds, secondaries, co-investment, real estate, real assets, infrastructure. LP/GP relationship data, fund performance benchmarks, and analyst research notes. ESG signals lighter than specialist vendors.

## 3. Datasets
- Coverage (approx., PitchBook-published): **~4.5m companies**, ~2.5m investments/deals, **525,000+ investors**, ~45,000 limited partners, **125,000+ funds**, ~4m people, plus advisor/service-provider and M&A data.
- **LCD:** the only real-time leveraged-loan/high-yield news+data provider; index families; distressed, middle-market, IG issuance, bankruptcy tracking.
- History depth: multi-decade deal/fund histories; strong US, growing global.
- **Sourcing method:** very large **human research team** (surveys, direct outreach to GPs/companies, primary calls) combined with machine collection from filings (SEC/FOIA), news, and web crawling; analyst-verified. Proprietary research reports (PE/VC quarterly, industry, emerging-tech) are a differentiator.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- **Delivery channels:** PitchBook Platform (web), **Excel Plugin**, **PitchBook Data Feed** (bulk/flat-file into internal systems/warehouses), and a **REST API** for programmatic access. LCD/Credit data accessible via platform, Excel Plugin, Data Feed, or API.
- **Premium Connectors:** brings PitchBook data into enterprise LLMs (Claude, ChatGPT) and **Microsoft 365 Copilot / Copilot in Excel** (natural-language target lists, diligence, screening).
- Auth token-based for API; formats JSON (API) / CSV-style bulk (Data Feed). Seat/named-user entitlement model; API and Data Feed licensed as separate paid modules.
- **Redistribution:** restricted — internal use licensing; external redistribution/display requires separate commercial terms.

## 5. Enabling technology
- Hybrid data engine: large-scale **human research operations** + machine ingestion (crawlers, filings parsing, NLP) with analyst verification — "human-verified" is an explicit marketing pillar.
- Entity resolution across millions of private companies/investors/people (dedup, relationship graph linking companies↔investors↔funds↔people↔deals).
- **AI/LLM features:** "data-first" AI strategy — PitchBook **Navigator** (in-platform AI), proprietary predictive models (**VC Exit Predictor**), and Premium Connectors/Copilot agents. Emphasis on grounding LLM output in human-verified data.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- **Ratings (triangulated):** TrustRadius ~517+ verified reviews, generally favorable (efficiency/time-savings praised); G2 listing exists (page egress-blocked in this run) but widely cited in the ~4.3/5 range across hundreds of reviews. Consistent themes across TrustRadius + buyer-guide sites.
- **PROS:** best-in-class **private-company / private-market data**; strong for BD, sourcing buyers/targets, fundraising tracking, benchmarking; useful news/research; big research-time savings; good Excel plugin.
- **CONS:** data can be **stale/incomplete** (revenue figures, contact details) for smaller/private names; search & filtering not always intuitive; primary research still needed for detailed diligence; **expensive** and opaque pricing; add-on modules inflate cost.
- **Segments:** PE/VC, investment banking, corporate development/M&A, consultants, lenders/private credit, academics.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- **Leads:** private-capital **breadth + depth** (VC/PE/M&A deals, valuations, cap tables, fund returns, LP/GP) — arguably the category leader for private-market deal data; reinforced by Morningstar distribution and **LCD** giving a credible leveraged-loan/private-credit franchise few private-market rivals match. Moat = scale of human research + relationship graph + proprietary research/indices.
- **Lags:** public-equity/real-time markets (Bloomberg, S&P Capital IQ, FactSet); LP-side fund-performance depth where **Preqin** is often preferred; niche-vertical/emerging-tech venture signals where **CB Insights** competes; occasional data-freshness gaps on long-tail private companies.
