# Trulioo

## 1. Snapshot
- Owner/parent: **Trulioo** (privately held; VC-backed, incl. TCV, Amex Ventures, Blumberg). HQ **Vancouver, Canada**; founded **2011**. Positioning: a global identity-verification network delivering KYC (person) and KYB (business) verification through a single API. Acquired **HelloFlow** (no-code onboarding/workflow builder, Copenhagen) in Feb 2022 to add end-to-end orchestration.
- **Regions/markets covered:** global — checks against government and other data sources across **195 countries**; broad coverage of both consumer and business verification worldwide.
- **Free tier:** No production free tier (sandbox/trial via sales; third parties report entry pricing). 
- Pricing model & known ranges: per-verification / volume-based enterprise quotes. Third-party-reported entry around **~$99/month** (not an official published rate); enterprise KYB/data-layer access quoted per volume.

## 2. Data-domain coverage
- Company reference/firmographics: yes — business verification with entity data on ~330M-700M business entities (figures vary by source).
- Beneficial ownership: yes — UBO/business-verification checks as part of KYB.
- KYC/identity: core strength — person verification, document verification, facial biometrics.
- Sanctions/PEP/adverse-media: yes — AML watchlist screening against 6,000+ watchlists.
- Private-company sourcing: no (not a deal-sourcing tool).
- Credit/risk scores: no (verification/compliance decisioning, not credit scoring).

## 3. Datasets
- **GlobalGateway** network: 450+ data sources, **5B+ consumers** and **330M-700M business entities** (source-dependent), **14,000+ document types**, and **6,000+ watchlists**, across **195 countries**. Method: aggregation of government registries, credit-header, telco, utility and proprietary data sources routed dynamically. No single proprietary universal ID; verification is match/confidence-based against underlying sources.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- **Single REST API** (GlobalGateway) for KYC, KYB, document verification and watchlist screening; JSON. Auth via API key. HelloFlow adds a no-code, drag-and-drop workflow/orchestration studio and low-code onboarding. Delivery is real-time (per-transaction) API; supports orchestration/routing across sources. SDKs for web/mobile capture. No public MCP server identified.

## 5. Enabling technology
- Dynamic source routing and normalization across 450+ sources; person-matching and identity-resolution enhancements; document authentication and facial-biometric liveness; AML watchlist/PEP screening; HelloFlow no-code workflow orchestration and onboarding automation; compliance tooling for KYC/KYB/AML programs.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- G2: **~4.4/5 (~40 reviews)**; also listed on Capterra, Gartner Peer Insights and SoftwareSuggest. Pros: sheer scale of global identity + business data, strong for KYB (not just individuals), responsive support/integration help. Cons: needs more engineering effort than document-first competitors, premium pricing, no-code UX sometimes weaker than rivals. User segments: fintech/payments compliance, banks, marketplaces, crypto — onboarding and AML teams.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- Leads on global breadth of identity + business verification through one integration, especially cross-border KYB. Lags on turnkey no-code polish and price versus document-first challengers. Best-for: platforms needing worldwide multi-country KYC/KYB coverage from a single API.
