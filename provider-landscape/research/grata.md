# Grata

## 1. Snapshot
- Owner/parent: **Datasite** (acquired Grata **3 June 2025**, deal reported >$200M; Datasite controlled by CapVest Partners, which committed **$500M** to expand intelligence solutions). Founded **2016, New York**; co-founders Andrew Bocskocsky and Nevin Raj continue to lead Grata as a strategic unit. Datasite is combining Grata with **Sourcescrub** (acquired Aug 2025).
- Positioning one-liner: AI-native private-market intelligence platform for dealmakers — search, source and research private companies (incl. founder-owned/bootstrapped) that traditional databases miss.
- **Regions/markets covered:** Strongest in **US**; expanded in **2025 into France, Germany, Australia and the UK**. Global company index with deeper firmographics in English-language / North American markets.
- **Free tier:** No (demo via sales; no advertised free tier).
- Pricing model & known ranges: Platform subscription + **credit-based API**; **not publicly disclosed** (enterprise SaaS; API priced per credit/call).

## 2. Data-domain coverage
- Company reference/firmographics: **core** — 12M+/21M+ private companies with 70+ fields (headcount, financials estimates, growth signals, industry, location).
- Beneficial ownership: no (not a UBO/registry product).
- KYC/identity: no.
- Sanctions/PEP/adverse-media: no.
- Private-company sourcing: **primary purpose** — AI-powered similar-company search, market mapping, sourcing, 10M+ executive contacts.
- Credit/risk scores: no bureau scores; provides growth indicators/estimates for prioritization.

## 3. Datasets
- Coverage counts: indexes **21M+ private companies** (12M+ deeply profiled with API access); **10M+ executive contacts**.
- Fields: **150+ data points** available via API; **70+ fields per company**.
- History depth: continuously refreshed AI-extracted data (freshness-oriented rather than long history).
- Proprietary IDs/graph: proprietary company index built by AI extraction; similarity/"search-by-description" engine at its core.
- Sourcing method: AI extraction from company websites + ML models + proprietary research/data science; claims ~99% data accuracy via extraction + validation.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- API: **credit-based REST company-data API** — 150+ data points across 12M+ companies (70+ fields each); search + enrichment endpoints.
- Auth: API key (credit consumption per call).
- Formats: JSON.
- Delivery: web platform, API, and **two-way CRM integrations** (Salesforce, DealCloud, Affinity, HubSpot, Dynamics, Altvia, Investorflow); partner integrations (e.g. BlueFlame AI).
- Batch vs real-time: real-time search/enrichment; list building and CRM sync.
- MCP availability: none published (partner AI integrations exist).

## 5. Enabling technology
- **AI-native search engine** ("the Engine") — natural-language / similar-company search over an AI-built private-company index.
- Entity/firmographic extraction and ML validation pipeline (claimed ~99% accuracy).
- Market-mapping, look-alike sourcing, and headcount/growth estimation models.
- Two-way CRM sync and workflow automation for deal pipelines.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- G2: **4.9/5 across ~81 reviews** (as listed on G2). Capterra: highly rated, praise for UI and data usability (smaller review base).
- Triangulated sentiment (G2, Capterra, Explorium review): **Pros** — excellent UI/UX, powerful AI similar-company search, coverage of hard-to-find founder-owned/non-sponsored companies, responsive/proactive support, strong CRM integration. **Cons** — non-US coverage still maturing, financials are estimates (not audited), enterprise pricing, contact-data depth varies.
- User segments: private equity, investment banking, corporate development, M&A advisors.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- Leads on: AI-native discovery of private/founder-owned companies invisible to PitchBook/Crunchbase; best-in-class search UX; now backed by Datasite scale + Sourcescrub data merge.
- Lags on: compliance/KYC/UBO and credit data (out of scope); non-US coverage depth; reliance on estimated financials.
- Best-for: dealmakers needing to source and map proprietary private-company targets quickly via AI search.
