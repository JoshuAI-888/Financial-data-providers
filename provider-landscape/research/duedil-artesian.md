# DueDil (FullCircl → now nCino Identity Solutions)

## 1. Snapshot
- Lineage/current status: DueDil (founded **2011, London**) merged with **Artesian Solutions** (2021) and the combined company **rebranded as FullCircl in March 2022** (Customer Lifecycle Intelligence). **FullCircl was acquired by nCino (NASDAQ: NCNO) in October 2024 for ~$135M**, and the DueDil/FullCircl product line is now branded **"nCino Identity Solutions."** DueDil's original API is now positioned as nCino KYB/KYC endpoints.
- Positioning one-liner: real-time B2B company + officer intelligence and KYB/KYC/onboarding for regulated financial services, powered by the proprietary Business Information Graph (B.I.G.).
- **Regions/markets covered:** Core strength **UK & Ireland** (6M+ companies with firmographic filters); global KYB reach across **160+ countries / ~365M entities** via nCino Identity Solutions. Weaker as a standalone US private-company tool.
- **Free tier:** No (trial/demo via sales).
- Pricing model & known ranges: Subscription/API-tiered; **not publicly disclosed** (enterprise SaaS + API call volume).

## 2. Data-domain coverage
- Company reference/firmographics: **core** — UK&I company profiles, directors, financials, group structures, red flags, mortgages/charges.
- Beneficial ownership: yes — ownership/group structure and UBO surfaced via the B.I.G.
- KYC/identity: yes — KYC/KYB, ID verification, fraud/AML checks (nCino Identity Solutions).
- Sanctions/PEP/adverse-media: yes — screening within onboarding/monitoring (often via partners/integrations).
- Private-company sourcing: partial — prospecting/"rich prospecting" (Artesian heritage: event monitoring, triggers, alerts), not a PE deal-sourcing tool.
- Credit/risk scores: yes — financial-risk indicators and growth signals on companies.

## 3. Datasets
- Business Information Graph (B.I.G.): analyzes **billions of data points daily**, surfacing **~270M+ connections** between companies and officers.
- Coverage: **6M+ UK & Ireland companies** with firmographic search; global KYB coverage cited at **365M entities across 160+ countries**.
- History depth: up to ~20 years of company financials (DueDil heritage).
- Proprietary IDs/graph: the B.I.G. is the proprietary entity graph linking companies, officers, ownership.
- Sourcing method: official registry data (Companies House etc.) + open-web unstructured data, cleaned/linked into the graph.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- API: REST (DueDil B.I.G. API → FullCircl / nCino KYB & KYC endpoints); documented at docs.fullcircl.com and developer.ncino.com.
- Auth: API key / OAuth (enterprise).
- Formats: JSON.
- Delivery: web application (SmartBroker / SmartOnboard heritage), API, and CRM/LOS integrations (Salesforce, nCino Bank Operating System for EMEA).
- Batch vs real-time: real-time lookups plus ongoing monitoring/alerting.
- MCP availability: none published.

## 5. Enabling technology
- Entity resolution + graph analytics (the B.I.G. connects companies/officers/ownership).
- Event/trigger monitoring and alerting (Artesian "actionable insight" heritage) over news and registry changes.
- AI/ML for data matching, deduplication, and prospecting signals.
- Compliance tooling: KYB/KYC onboarding, perpetual/ongoing monitoring, rules-based risk workflows.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- Public standalone review volume is thin post-rebrand/acquisition (product now folded into nCino). Company-reported traction: worked with ~40% of the UK's top financial institutions and 7 of the top 10 UK banks; reached EBITDA profitability post-merger.
- Triangulated sentiment (Crunchbase/CB Insights profiles, vendor + press): **Pros** — best-in-class UK&I company/officer data, single-API onboarding, strong graph-based linkage. **Cons** — UK-centric depth (thinner outside UK&I standalone), enterprise-only pricing, brand churn (DueDil→FullCircl→nCino) creating continuity uncertainty.
- User segments: banks, insurers, fintechs, brokers — onboarding/compliance and commercial teams.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- Leads on: UK & Ireland company intelligence and regulated-onboarding KYB/KYC via the B.I.G.; now backed by nCino's banking-platform distribution.
- Lags on: US/global private-company sourcing depth, brand stability, and public pricing transparency.
- Best-for: UK/EMEA banks and insurers wanting registry-grade company + officer data embedded into KYB/KYC onboarding.
