# RepRisk

## 1. Snapshot
- **Owner/parent:** Independent Swiss company, headquartered in Zurich (founded 1998/2006 lineage as an ESG risk-research pioneer), with offices in Toronto, New York, London, Berlin, Manila, and Tokyo; distributed/embedded through partners including FactSet and (per 2026 news) Fitch Group; no controlling parent identified in available sources.
- **Coverage:** 310,000–350,000+ public and private companies across every sector and market (developed and emerging), plus projects/infrastructure entities; data drawn from 100,000+ media, stakeholder, and third-party sources daily.
- **Free tier:** no — subscription/license-based B2B data product; no self-serve free tier identified.
- **Pricing model & ranges:** Subscription-based, annual or monthly license fees; no public price list — quote-based, scaled by data volume/entities and delivery method (platform, API, or flat files).

## 2. Coverage
310,000+ (RepRisk itself cites 350,000+ in some materials) public and private companies plus infrastructure/development projects, spanning both developed and emerging markets and every sector — explicitly the largest reported entity database among ESG risk vendors. Coverage is "outside-in": built entirely from external public-source monitoring (media, NGOs, regulators, social media, government bodies) rather than company self-disclosure, so it does not depend on issuer reporting quality/willingness and can cover private/unlisted entities that disclosure-based providers cannot.

## 3. Datasets
Core products: the **RepRisk Index (RRI)**, a dynamic, daily-updated score quantifying a company's or project's exposure to ESG and business-conduct reputational risk; the **RepRisk Rating (RRR)**, which combines RRI with sector/country risk context; incident-level qualitative research on individual controversies (severity, reach, novelty); and a **RepRisk Data Feed** for bulk/systematic integration. Also produces thematic research (e.g., the 2026 "Business Conduct Risk Intelligence Report" co-published with Oxford Economics) covering rising business-conduct risks in financial services.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
REST API for real-time programmatic access to RepRisk data, plus scheduled daily/weekly flat-file delivery (Excel, CSV) tailored to client-defined identifiers and watchlists. Distributed through third-party platforms including FactSet's RepRisk Data Feed integration and (announced 2026) a Fitch Group partnership; built for embedding into existing risk/due-diligence/portfolio-monitoring systems rather than as a standalone terminal.

## 5. Enabling technology
Hybrid AI + human-analyst pipeline: machine-learning models screen and flag documents from 100,000+ daily-monitored sources, appending relevance predictions, then a team of 150+ human ESG analysts reviews and validates every risk incident before it is published — RepRisk explicitly markets this "AI + human intelligence" combination as a differentiator over pure-AI or pure-analyst competitors, arguing it avoids both AI hallucination/noise and the scale limits of manual-only research.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
No detailed Gartner Peer Insights review text was surfaced in available sources (listing exists but review content wasn't retrievable here). Third-party commentary (Citi Global Data Insights research, cited by RepRisk) reportedly finds potential for investment outperformance associated with RepRisk data. Positioning commentary from independent analysts consistently frames RepRisk as the specialist "outside-in"/controversy-detection vendor, distinct from and often complementary to disclosure-based raters like MSCI and Sustainalytics, which is echoed by clients using it for due-diligence screening and early risk-escalation workflows.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- **Leads on:** Largest reported entity database (310k–350k+, including private/unlisted companies and projects); pure outside-in, disclosure-independent controversy detection; large human-analyst layer (150+) validating every AI-flagged incident; daily update cadence.
- **Lags on:** No underlying disclosure-based/fundamental ESG scoring (it doesn't replace a full ESG ratings suite); no public pricing/free tier; narrower thematic scope (reputational/business-conduct risk) vs. broader ESG data platforms like Clarity AI or MSCI.
- **Best-for:** Real-time reputational/controversy screening, adverse-media due diligence, and portfolio risk-escalation workflows — typically used alongside, not instead of, a disclosure-based ESG ratings provider.
