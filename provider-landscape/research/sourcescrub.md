# Sourcescrub

## 1. Snapshot
- Owner/parent: **Datasite** (acquisition announced **8 Aug 2025**, from Francisco Partners; Datasite controlled by CapVest Partners). Founded **2015, San Francisco, CA**. Being merged with sibling acquisition **Grata** under Datasite's private-market intelligence unit.
- Positioning one-liner: deal-sourcing / private-company intelligence platform that mines conference lists, "best-of" lists and industry directories to surface founder-owned, bootstrapped, lower- and middle-market companies for PE, IB, corp dev and consulting.
- **Regions/markets covered:** Global company universe with strongest depth in **US** and developed markets; growing EU coverage. Sourcing method (events/directories) skews to markets with rich conference/trade-show ecosystems.
- **Free tier:** No (demo via sales; no advertised free trial).
- Pricing model & known ranges: Subscription (seat/module based); **not publicly disclosed**. Reviewers describe it as premium-priced ("not the cheapest, worth it if used regularly").

## 2. Data-domain coverage
- Company reference/firmographics: **core** — 16M+ deeply profiled private companies with contacts, headcount, growth signals.
- Beneficial ownership: no (not a UBO/registry tool).
- KYC/identity: no.
- Sanctions/PEP/adverse-media: no.
- Private-company sourcing: **primary purpose** — target-list building, market mapping, conference/attendee intelligence.
- Credit/risk scores: no bureau scores; provides growth/signal metrics for prioritization.

## 3. Datasets
- Coverage counts: **16M+ private companies**; data cleaned/connected from **~290,000 sources** (product reviews, best-of lists, trade shows, conference/attendee lists, industry orgs, portfolio lists, news, websites).
- Conference/event depth: **190,000+ scrubbed conference & trade-show lists** — a signature differentiator.
- History depth: continuously refreshed; emphasis on freshness over long time series.
- Proprietary IDs/graph: interconnected company universe linking companies to sources, signals and contacts.
- Sourcing method: "Expert-in-the-Loop" ML — automated extraction plus human review/verification.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- Delivery: web platform plus CRM integrations (Salesforce, DealCloud, Affinity and others) for two-way list/pipeline sync; data exports.
- API: company/data API and enrichment available to customers (credit/entitlement based); Datasite integration expanding post-acquisition.
- Auth: API key / enterprise credentials.
- Formats: JSON / CSV export.
- Batch vs real-time: on-demand search + list building; enrichment and CRM sync.
- MCP availability: none published.

## 5. Enabling technology
- "Expert-in-the-Loop" ML pipeline combining automated scraping/extraction with human verification for accuracy/freshness.
- Signal detection (conference presence, list appearances, growth indicators) for target prioritization.
- Market-mapping and target-list automation; an AI Agent product sheet (2025) for automated target-list creation.
- Data-ops around continuous source ingestion and deduplication.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- G2: **4.5/5 across ~56 reviews**; named a Leader in G2's M&A and Financial Research Software categories. Capterra: highly rated (4.5+ range) with a smaller review base.
- Triangulated sentiment (G2, Capterra, Research.com): **Pros** — unique conference/event-sourced data, strong lower/middle-market founder-owned coverage, accurate data, responsive support, good CRM integration. **Cons** — premium pricing; marginal value if used infrequently; coverage skewed to event-heavy sectors.
- User segments: PE, investment banking, corporate development, management consulting deal teams.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- Leads on: proprietary conference/directory-sourced discovery of hard-to-find private companies missed by PitchBook/Crunchbase; market mapping.
- Lags on: no compliance/KYC/UBO or credit data; pricing; coverage thinner outside event-rich industries.
- Best-for: PE/IB deal-origination teams building proprietary target lists of founder-owned lower/mid-market companies.
