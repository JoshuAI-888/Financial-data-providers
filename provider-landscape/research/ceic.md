# CEIC (ISI Emerging Markets Group)

## 1. Snapshot
- **Owner/parent:** CEIC Data is part of **ISI Emerging Markets Group** (brand: ISI Markets / isimarkets.com). ISI was owned by Euromoney Institutional Investor, then sold in 2019 to **CITIC Capital and Caixin Global**. Founded **1992**. CEIC's registered/operating HQ is London; ISI's group HQ is Hong Kong, with offices across ~18 countries.
- **Positioning one-liner:** The go-to premium database for **emerging-market macro depth**, consolidating official statistics, local-source series and alternative indicators into standardised, comparable country time series.
- **Regions/coverage:** Global but **EM-focused and Asia-strong** (its heritage is China/Asia). Deep local-source coverage across Asia (China, India, ASEAN), CEE/CIS, LatAm, Middle East and Africa, plus developed markets. NZ/AU covered, but the differentiator is granular EM/local-agency data others don't carry.
- **Free tier:** No — subscription only (demo on request). Some indicator snapshots are visible free on ceicdata.com, but the database and API are paid.
- **Pricing model & known ranges:** Enterprise subscription, module/seat-based; **not publicly disclosed** (demo/quote required). Competitor intel puts it in the same premium bracket as Haver (mid five- to six-figure annual for institutional seats).

## 2. Data-domain coverage
- **Macro indicators:** Yes — national accounts, prices, labour, demographics, government/public finance, investment, monetary.
- **Central-bank/rates:** Yes — interest rates, FX, monetary aggregates, financial-market series.
- **Trade:** Yes — domestic trade, foreign trade, balance of payments (deep, often sub-national/commodity-level).
- **Commodities:** Yes — via industry/sector databases and commodity-linked indicators (heavy China industrial detail).
- **Forecasts:** Limited — CEIC's edge is source data and nowcasting inputs, not a proprietary forecast suite (some indicator forecasts/consensus surfaced).
- **Country risk:** Partial — comparative macro/industry indicators support risk work rather than a packaged rating product.
- **High-frequency/alt-macro:** Yes — 500,000+ high-frequency and nowcasting datasets plus alternative indicators to spot inflection points; a stated strength.

## 3. Datasets
- **Millions of macroeconomic time series** across **200+ countries/economies**; marketed 500,000+ high-frequency/nowcasting datasets on top of the core macro database, plus industry/sector (China/EM) and alternative data.
- History depth extends decades where sources allow; strength is coverage of **local statistical agencies and central banks** others don't ingest.
- Update frequency ranges daily/weekly/monthly to source cadence; standardised at country level for cross-market comparability.
- Sourcing: official statistics + proprietary/curated + alternative indicators; heavy analyst curation of local sources.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- **API type:** REST **CEIC Macroeconomic Data API** (on developer.isimarkets.com); plus a **CEIC API SDK**, Excel/desktop platform (CDMNext web app), Python/R support and a database-manager plugin.
- **Auth:** Subscription credentials / token via the developer portal.
- **Formats:** JSON via API; Excel/CSV export from the platform.
- **Rate limits:** Governed by subscription; not publicly published.
- **Delivery:** API, web platform (CDMNext), Excel add-in, bulk/database feeds; no publicly advertised Snowflake/Databricks native share or MCP server.

## 5. Enabling technology
- Core value is **harmonisation of heterogeneous local sources** into consistent, comparable country time series with unit/frequency standardisation.
- Metadata-rich series with source lineage; revisions tracked as sources restate (point-in-time depth less emphasised than Macrobond/ALFRED).
- Analyst-curated collection and quality control of local-language/local-agency data; growing nowcasting and alternative-data layer.
- Platform-level charting, transformation and dataset management (CDMNext).

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- Sentiment (competitor/analyst commentary, research citations): regarded as **best-in-class for EM and China/Asia depth** and local-source breadth; frequently cited alongside Haver in institutional EM research.
- Cons: premium price, and coverage/UX can feel dense; some users pair it with Haver/Macrobond rather than replace them. Detailed public user reviews are scarce (enterprise, gated product).
- User segments: EM economists, sovereign/credit and macro strategists, buy-side and sell-side research, central banks, governments, corporates and academics.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- **Leads on:** emerging-market and Asia/China local-source depth, granular industry and high-frequency/alternative EM data.
- **Lags on:** price/accessibility, packaged forecasting/country-risk products, and public transparency versus free official sources.
- **Best for:** institutions that need the deepest, most granular EM/Asia macro and industry data in one harmonised platform.
- **Free pick?** No — a premium, enterprise EM specialist.
