# With Intelligence

## 1. Snapshot
- **Owner/parent:** **S&P Global** — agreed to acquire With Intelligence from majority owner **Motive Partners** for **~$1.8bn** (announced 2025); data being integrated into **S&P Capital IQ Pro** through 2026. HQ London; roots to 1998 (founder Charlie Kerr, originally HFM). Offices London, NYC, Hong Kong.
- **Scale signal:** One of the industry's largest databases of alternative fund managers + allocators; strong events/editorial franchise (HFM, The Deal). Serves allocators, managers, IR/DD teams, consultants.
- **Positioning one-liner:** "Data, intelligence and events for the alternatives and wealth business" — fund + **allocator** intelligence spanning hedge funds, private markets and wealth.
- **Pricing model:** Not publicly disclosed; subscription by dataset/module + seats, plus events/awards revenue. (Ranges not published.)

## 2. Asset-class coverage
- **Public equity:** Not a focus — alternatives/allocator specialist.
- **Private equity / VC:** Covered — private equity, **private credit**, real assets fund/manager/investor data; strengthened via partner datasets (Cambridge Associates, Mercer, S&P) for private credit and real assets.
- **Fixed income & credit:** Private credit as an alternatives asset class (not public/liquid FI).
- **Other (funds/LP-GP, real assets, ESG, alt data):** Core strength — **hedge funds** (Eurekahedge, HFM indices), real assets, and the **wealth/allocator** segment. Signature differentiator: **direct-from-allocator commitment/allocation data** (capital commitments recorded directly from LPs, not manager-reported).

## 3. Datasets
- Databases/brands under one roof: **Eurekahedge** (leading hedge-fund data, strong Asia/EM coverage + indices), **HFM** (Hedge Fund Monitor indices/intelligence), **SPS** (private markets), **Highworth Research** (family offices), **The Deal** (editorial).
- Coverage: one of the largest alt-manager databases + broad allocator/investor coverage; direct-from-investor allocation & commitment records; fund performance and forward-looking editorial. (Exact fund/allocator counts not consistently published.)
- **Sourcing method:** research/editorial teams + **direct-from-allocator surveying** and manager reporting; index construction (Eurekahedge/HFM); events-driven relationship sourcing.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- **With HFM data service** and dataset feeds; delivery to enterprise systems (feeds/files). Data now surfaced through **S&P Capital IQ Pro** (investor allocation/commitment data, fund performance, fund-level screening, The Deal editorial).
- API/feed specifics less publicly documented than PitchBook/Preqin; delivery historically via platform + data feeds; entitlement by dataset + seats. Redistribution under enterprise terms; S&P integration expands distribution.

## 5. Enabling technology
- Research- and editorial-led operation (analysts, direct allocator outreach, events) rather than an AI-first platform; index engines for Eurekahedge/HFM.
- Entity resolution across managers/funds/allocators; benchmarking/peer-analysis tooling.
- Post-acquisition: technology and distribution leverage from **S&P Global / Capital IQ Pro** (standardized, aggregated multi-source datasets).

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- **Ratings (triangulated):** Less represented on G2/TrustRadius than PitchBook/Preqin/CB Insights; sentiment drawn from hedge-fund-database comparison guides (gitnux, bestfinancesites) and industry/press coverage — regarded as a top hedge-fund/allocator data source, especially for **allocator sourcing** and **Asia/EM hedge funds** (Eurekahedge).
- **PROS:** unique **direct-from-allocator** commitment data; deep hedge-fund + allocator coverage; strong regional (Asia/EM) hedge-fund data via Eurekahedge; respected indices; valuable events/awards + editorial (The Deal, HFM).
- **CONS:** less transactional/deal-level depth than PitchBook; thinner independent review-site presence; historically fragmented across acquired brands (integration ongoing); pricing opaque; not a public-markets or broad PE-deal source.
- **Segments:** hedge-fund allocators, capital-introduction/IR teams, fund marketers/sales, consultants, wealth/private-markets allocators.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- **Leads:** **allocator intelligence + hedge-fund data** — the "who is allocating and to whom" angle via direct-from-allocator commitment data, plus Eurekahedge/HFM hedge-fund coverage and events/editorial reach. Moat = allocator relationships + regional hedge-fund depth + events ecosystem; now amplified by S&P Global distribution.
- **Lags:** private-company/deal granularity vs **PitchBook**; fund-performance/LP-GP breadth vs **Preqin**; venture/emerging-tech signals vs **CB Insights**; weaker standalone API/tech story and lighter public review footprint.
