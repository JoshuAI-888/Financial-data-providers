# IDP Vendor Evidence Refresh — v3 Updates (Nov 2026)

**As of:** 2026-11-03. **Previous refresh:** `_v2_updates.md` (2026-08-14).

**Scope.**
- The five vendors that missed the August refresh (State Street Alpha, Quadra, Fencore, AlphaCert,
  Exafluence) got a full audit against the same five gaps: (1) Databricks & open architecture,
  (2) AI / agents / MCP, (3) ownership, (4) APAC / ANZ, (5) pricing. Window: 2024-01 to 2026-11-03.
- The other ten vendors were updated for 2026-08-14 to 2026-11-03, starting with the priority leads
  (AustralianSuper on FINBOURNE, Arcesium Intelligence, FactSet and MSCI in Gemini Enterprise, the
  Rest/Rimes confirmation, FINBOURNE Yield Book, NeoXam preview items). Items the August pass missed
  were backfilled where found.

**Method.** One Sonnet research agent per vendor, working to the brief in `_research_brief.md`, used
web search plus page fetches. Every new source is dated and tagged `vendor` or `independent`, and
`opened` (fetched and read) or `search-only`. All findings and every score or gate change were
reviewed by Opus before entering the notes or `reqscores_*.json`. Several proposed changes were
rejected or reduced (listed below). Scores remain a **Stage-1 paper screen, provisional until a
proof of value runs**, and stay within the evidence caps (roadmap ≤1.0, sales ≤2.0, docs ≤3.0,
demo ≤3.5, named-customer ≤4.0).

**Baseline reconciliation (no score change).** Before this refresh, seven cells in
`reqscores_new_b.json` and `reqscores_new_d.json` differed from the published page. The page had
been hand-edited, and its values were the reviewed ones. The JSON now matches the page: Quadra
DQ-04, CUR-01 and CUR-04 at 0.5; AlphaCert CUR-01 0.5; Fencore CUR-03 0.5; Exafluence CUR-03 0.5;
State Street VIA-03 3.0. From now on, pages are generated by `tools/build_pages.py`, and domain
scores are computed from requirement scores, not typed.
