# Tegus

## 1. Snapshot
- **Owner/parent:** **AlphaSense** (acquired Tegus; announced Jun 2024, closed Jul 8 2024, ~$930M). Now branded "Tegus by AlphaSense." **HQ:** Chicago, IL. **Founded:** 2017 by Michael Elnick and Tom Elnick. **Positioning:** Investor-led expert-interview library plus financial models (Canalyst) and SEC filings (BamSEC) — a "read, don't dial" alternative to traditional expert networks.
- **M&A:** As acquirer — bought **BamSEC** (Oct 2021) and **Canalyst** (Aug 2022). As target — acquired by AlphaSense (2024).
- **Regions/markets covered:** Global. Expert interviews and Canalyst models span 35,000+ public and private companies, US-heavy but with meaningful international coverage.
- **Free tier:** No — institutional subscription only (trials via sales).
- **Pricing model & known ranges:** Flat annual subscription for unrestricted access (transcripts + Canalyst models + BamSEC), rather than per-call; expert calls billed **at cost** (no markup). Segmented by AUM (SMB <$500M, mid-market, enterprise $1–5B, strategic >$5B). Specific figures **not publicly disclosed**; historically marketed as materially cheaper per-transcript than legacy expert networks.

## 2. Data-domain coverage
- **fundamentals:** Yes — Canalyst standardized financial models and company data.
- **filings:** Yes — via BamSEC SEC-filing search and earnings-call transcripts.
- **transcripts/expert calls:** Core — 150,000+ investor-led expert-interview transcripts (100,000+ historically cited), library-first model where transcripts are shared across the client base.
- **qualitative research:** Yes — expert interviews with former execs, customers, competitors, suppliers.
- **private-company data:** Yes — expert coverage of private companies within the 35,000+ universe.
- **entity/knowledge graph:** No standalone graph; searchable/tagged transcript + model corpus.

## 3. Datasets
- Expert-interview transcript library (150,000+ transcripts; 35,000+ companies), Canalyst financial models, BamSEC filings/transcripts.
- **Sourcing method:** Proprietary expert network conducting investor-led calls that are transcribed and added to a shared library; models built/maintained by Canalyst analysts; filings ingested by BamSEC.
- **Depth/history:** Multi-year transcript archive; Canalyst models with historical + forecast lines.
- **Proprietary holdings:** The shared expert-transcript corpus (its core moat) and Canalyst model library — both now folded into AlphaSense.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- Primarily a web platform; AI-powered search across transcripts, models and filings.
- Data/API access increasingly delivered via the parent **AlphaSense** developer platform and Agent/MCP tooling post-merger; Canalyst offered model data feeds.
- Excel/model access via Canalyst; BamSEC allows table downloads and linking financials into user models.
- **AI features:** Search copilots and summarization inherited/aligned with AlphaSense Assistant.

## 5. Enabling technology
- Human expert-network operations (sourcing, compliance screening, call facilitation) plus transcription/NLP tagging for search.
- Canalyst's analyst-built model methodology (human-in-the-loop) for standardized fundamentals.
- Post-acquisition, benefits from AlphaSense's semantic search and GenAI stack.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- **Ratings:** Trustpilot reviews (tegus.com) generally positive on professionalism/responsiveness; G2 has very few product reviews; Glassdoor ~246 employee reviews (workplace, not product). Triangulates to "well-regarded content, thin formal product-review sample."
- **Pros:** Shared transcript library (read many calls without commissioning each), calls at cost, fast turnaround, strong buy-side content.
- **Cons:** Investor-only access, some coverage gaps outside core sectors, Q4 2023 layoffs (~10–15%) signaled slower-than-expected growth; product identity now merging into AlphaSense.
- **User segments:** Hedge funds, mutual funds, VC/PE and other buy-side investors.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- **Leads:** Pioneered the transcript-library / calls-at-cost model that undercut legacy expert networks; deep buy-side expert content plus bundled models + filings.
- **Lags:** Investor-only, less suited to corporate/consulting buyers than Third Bridge/GLG; now a brand inside AlphaSense rather than a standalone roadmap.
- **Best for:** Public-market buy-side analysts wanting large volumes of expert transcripts plus ready-made models and filings under one flat fee.
