# Clarity AI

## 1. Snapshot
- **Owner/parent:** Independent, VC-backed sustainability-tech company (founded 2017, HQ in New York/Madrid); investors include SoftBank Investment Advisers (Vision Fund 2), Balderton Capital, Fifth Wall, State Street, BlackRock, and Deutsche Börse (per PitchBook/CB Insights); not owned by a larger index/data incumbent, though it partners closely with LSEG, BlackRock (Aladdin), CACEIS, and others for distribution.
- **Coverage:** 60,000–70,000+ companies, 390,000–420,000+ funds/ETFs (look-through), 201 countries, 199 local governments/sovereigns.
- **Free tier:** no — enterprise SaaS/data-licensing product aimed at institutional investors, banks, and corporates; no self-serve free tier identified.
- **Pricing model & ranges:** Subscription-based with modular/tiered licensing — price varies by number of modules (e.g., SFDR PAI reporting, carbon, controversies), data volume/entity count, and delivery method (web app vs. API/data feed); no public price list, quote-based.

## 2. Coverage
Broad multi-asset sustainability-data coverage: 60,000–70,000+ listed and private companies, 390,000–420,000+ funds and ETFs with portfolio look-through, plus 201 countries and 199 local governments/municipalities for sovereign ESG. Data blends company self-disclosure, third-party feeds (e.g., CDP), and AI-based estimation to fill gaps where disclosure is absent — a hybrid disclosure-plus-estimation model rather than RepRisk's pure outside-in controversy approach.

## 3. Datasets
Core sustainability/ESG scores and ratings; EU SFDR Principal Adverse Impact (PAI) indicators — covering all 46 mandatory PAIs (14 for companies, plus sovereign/real-estate PAIs) plus 30+ voluntary ones; carbon emissions/footprint and intensity metrics; EU Taxonomy alignment; climate risk (physical and transition, enhanced via a 2024 partnership with RiskThinking.ai for asset-level climate risk); controversy/norms-based screening; biodiversity and nature-related data; and, following the 2025 acquisition of ecolytiq, consumer-facing/embedded sustainability data for banking-transaction carbon footprinting.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
Delivered via web application, REST API endpoints, and pre-built connectors for third-party workflows (PMS/OMS, client portals, regulatory reporting tools). Notable integrations include BlackRock's Aladdin (enterprise SFDR reporting), LSEG (joint SFDR compliance tooling), CACEIS, and Next Gate Tech. Positioned for embedding sustainability metrics directly into existing investment and reporting infrastructure rather than as a standalone terminal only.

## 5. Enabling technology
AI/ML-driven estimation engine used to fill ESG data gaps where company disclosure is absent or delayed (a key differentiator vs. disclosure-only providers), combined with methodology transparency (the vendor emphasizes showing its full calculation "working," similar in spirit to this repo's own transform.py explainability requirement). Recognized as a Leader in The Forrester Wave: ESG Data & Analytics Providers (Q3 2024) and named "Best Overall ESG Tech Provider" in the ESG Insight Awards.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
No public G2/Gartner Peer Insights review volume was found (Gartner lists the product with no customer reviews at time of research). Market recognition comes chiefly from analyst reports (Forrester Wave Leader) and high-profile partnership/distribution deals (BlackRock Aladdin, LSEG, CACEIS) signaling institutional trust and platform embedding. Marketed strength is regulatory-reporting workflow (SFDR PAI) and AI-based gap-filling versus peers who report more data as "not available" where disclosure is missing.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- **Leads on:** AI-based ESG data-gap estimation/imputation, EU SFDR PAI regulatory-reporting completeness (46+30 PAIs), deep fund/ETF look-through (390k+ funds), embedded distribution via BlackRock Aladdin/LSEG.
- **Lags on:** No self-serve free tier or transparent public pricing; newer/independent brand vs. legacy incumbents (MSCI, Sustainalytics, ISS ESG) with longer institutional track records; disclosure-plus-estimation approach can be seen as less "outside-in"/independent than RepRisk on controversy detection.
- **Best-for:** Asset managers and banks needing EU regulatory (SFDR/Taxonomy) sustainability reporting with maximum data completeness via AI estimation, embedded into existing PMS/OMS/Aladdin workflows.
