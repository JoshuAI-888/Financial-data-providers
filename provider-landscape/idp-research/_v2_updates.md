## Cross-vendor summary of the most material NEW findings

- **Databricks-native data access is now a live differentiator for three vendors:** Rimes (Delta Sharing, 18 Nov 2025), and Arcesium + FactSet (Databricks **MCP Marketplace** launch partners, Nov 2025). MSCI, SimCorp, NeoXam, Gresham/Opus, GoldenSource and BlackRock Aladdin show **no** Databricks/Delta Sharing integration this pass (SimCorp and GoldenSource are on Azure and Amazon Bedrock respectively).
- **MCP / agent GA momentum:** FINBOURNE (MCP + Claude, ~Jun 2025), FactSet (MCP server, model-independent across Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini/Copilot/Databricks etc.), and Arcesium (MCP on Databricks) are the clearest MCP stories. SimCorp **Agent Launchpad** (Apr 2026, rolling GA) and BlackRock **Aladdin Copilot** (now GA to all clients) are the big embedded-agent moves — but both appear vendor-controlled-LLM, not buyer-own-LLM. FactSet and MSCI are the strongest explicit **model-independence** signals.
- **Corporate change:** Gresham **acquired S&P Global's EDM business and rebranded it "Opus EDM"** (completed ~Jan 2026); BlackRock **closed the Preqin acquisition** (3 Mar 2025) and is folding Preqin data into Aladdin; FactSet↔Arcesium struck a **strategic partnership** (Dec 2025). Ownership otherwise unchanged (Rimes/Rothschild-Five Arrows, NeoXam/Eurazeo, GoldenSource/Gemspring, SimCorp/Deutsche Börse).
- **APAC/ANZ gap closures:** Rimes now has a named ANZ client (**Rest**, A$100bn super, Matrix, Apr 2026); NeoXam has **Sydney (2024) + Melbourne (2025)** offices and named ANZ clients (Platinum Asset Management). FINBOURNE is expanding into Australia/Singapore. FactSet ran a Sydney APAC buy-side forum. SimCorp/MSCI/BlackRock/Gresham/GoldenSource still lack specific ANZ references in this pass.
- **Pricing:** still overwhelmingly undisclosed. The one exception is **FactSet**, for which third-party procurement aggregators (Vendr, Costbench) publish per-user estimate ranges (~$4k–$50k+/user/yr) — indicative only, not official list pricing.
