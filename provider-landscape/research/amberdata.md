# Amberdata

## 1. Snapshot
- **Owner/parent:** Independent, VC-backed (investors include Coinbase Ventures, Nasdaq Ventures, Boldstart). Founder/CEO **Shawn Douglass**. HQ **New York, NY** (also Palo Alto roots). **Founded 2017**. Positioning: institutional digital-asset infrastructure spanning market, blockchain and DeFi/derivatives data plus analytics.
- **Coverage:** Spot + derivatives across major exchanges, deep options analytics, DeFi/DEX and lending protocols, and on-chain data across leading blockchains (blocks, txns, addresses, tokens, transfers). Raised ~$47M total (Series B $30M, 2022).
- **Free tier:** **Yes (limited).** Free developer plan/API key with capped requests for evaluation; production/institutional volumes require paid plans. (Flag: free tier is evaluation-scale, not production.)
- **Pricing model & ranges:** Paid tiers listed on amberdata.io/pricing but **specific figures not publicly disclosed** here; scales from developer plans to bespoke enterprise (API + bulk/cloud delivery). Enterprise pricing by quote.

## 2. Data-domain coverage
- **Market data (spot/derivatives):** Yes — prices, OHLCV, tickers, trades, order books; futures + options (funding, OI, liquidations, IV, Greeks).
- **On-chain:** Yes — blockchain network data (blocks, transactions, addresses, tokens, transfers, hash rate, fees, supply).
- **Reference/metadata:** Yes — asset/instrument reference and network metrics.
- **Indices:** Limited — reference/pricing services rather than a headline regulated index franchise.
- **DeFi:** Yes — strong DEX trades, lending, and one of the broadest **DeFi options vaults/DEX** coverage sets.
- **Research/analytics:** Yes — AmberLens and AD Derivatives analytics, volatility surfaces, research.
- **Institutional/regulatory-grade:** Yes — positioned as institutional-grade with enterprise delivery and pricing services.

## 3. Datasets
- Spot and derivatives market data (trades, order books, OHLCV), **AD Derivatives** options analytics (live + historical, volatility surfaces, strikes, moneyness), DeFi/DEX and lending data, and full blockchain on-chain datasets.
- History depth spans multi-year market and on-chain archives; granularity from tick/trade and order-book snapshots to on-chain event level.
- Proprietary holdings: AD Derivatives options analytics and AmberLens market-intelligence layer are notable differentiators, especially for crypto options desks.

## 4. APIs & technical integration
- **API types:** RESTful API (api.amberdata.com), low-latency **WebSocket** streaming, and **flat-file / bulk** delivery.
- **Delivery:** REST + WebSocket feeds, **AWS S3** bulk downloads, and cloud-warehouse delivery via **Snowflake, Databricks, and BigQuery** (Snowflake Marketplace listing).
- **Auth/formats:** API-key auth; JSON over REST/WebSocket; bulk flat files for history.
- **Rate limits:** Plan-dependent; free/developer tier capped, enterprise negotiated.
- **MCP availability:** No official Amberdata MCP server identified as of Aug 2026.

## 5. Enabling technology
- Normalises spot, derivatives, DeFi and on-chain data into unified schemas; strong emphasis on **derivatives/options analytics** (Greeks, IV surfaces) built by trading practitioners.
- On-chain indexing across major networks (state/health metrics, address- and token-level activity).
- Data-ops: multi-modal delivery (API/WebSocket/S3/Snowflake/Databricks) supports both real-time trading and warehouse-based quant/backtesting workflows.

## 6. Customer / user feedback
- Triangulated (Amberdata product pages, Datarade, Web3Connect, GitHub API docs): regarded as a **strong institutional all-rounder**, singled out for **crypto options/derivatives analytics** and DeFi breadth.
- **Pros:** comprehensive derivatives + DeFi + on-chain in one platform; flexible enterprise delivery; backed by Coinbase/Nasdaq.
- **Cons:** pricing opacity; free tier limited; less of a household name than CoinGecko/CMC for retail; index franchise less prominent than Kaiko/CCData.
- **Segments:** institutional trading desks, quant funds, options market makers, DeFi analytics teams.

## 7. Edge & positioning
- **Leads on:** unified **derivatives (options) + DeFi + on-chain** institutional coverage with AD Derivatives analytics; multi-cloud delivery (Snowflake/Databricks).
- **Lags on:** public price transparency and regulated-benchmark prominence; retail reach.
- **Best-for:** institutional desks needing crypto options/derivatives analytics alongside DeFi and on-chain data. **Institutional-grade**, with only an evaluation-scale free tier.
